  What's built
  - Players: each gets a random seeded role and a pseudonymous ID. Everyone sees
    the same live board, and each player's private page is only sent to that
    player. Joining again on the same device keeps the same seat.
  - Rules enforced:
    - Funding is exact (owned cost, or the top of the pipeline range).
    - No chips go on scouting companies, and the bank can't be overspent.
    - Counters can only go on their own row, at most 2 per company, from a
      shared pool of 3 per type.
    - The 3 chips owed next round only produce a warning.
  - Round flow:
    - The board is locked during the 3-minute reading, then opens.
    - It freezes automatically at 20:00, or when the facilitator freezes it, or
      when all four agree to finish early. Any board change resets the
      early-finish votes.
  - Facilitator console: it shows the timer, seats and live board. It can start
    the round and freeze it, and replace a participant between rounds (the new
    person gets a new ID and the role history stays in the log). It also exports
    the two CSVs.
  - Logging: every board change (who, what, before and after), first opening of
    each private page, round start, freeze and early-finish votes, and the final
    board are timestamped. Export is tidy long-format CSV: all events, plus the
    final boards alone.
  - Deployment readiness:
    - Settings come from environment variables.
    - URLs are relative, and the WebSocket scheme follows the page's protocol.
    - There is a /healthz endpoint.
    - Sessions and the timer survive a server restart.
